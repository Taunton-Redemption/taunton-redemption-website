# Taunton Redemption Center Website

A one-page public site for a Massachusetts redemption center, telling customers whether the shop is open and what containers and rules apply.

## Language

**Redemption center**:
A shop that takes back empty deposit containers from customers and pays out the deposit, less any processing fee it chooses to deduct.
_Avoid_: Recycling center, bottle shop

**Deposit container**:
A can or bottle that earns the 5¢ Massachusetts deposit: carbonated soft drinks, beer, malt beverages, sparkling water.
_Avoid_: Returnable, recyclable

**Non-deposit container**:
A container that earns no deposit, such as water, juice, iced tea, wine, or liquor bottles. The shop does not accept these.
_Avoid_: Non-returnable

**Daily bottle limit**:
The most loose deposit glass bottles of alcoholic drinks the shop accepts from one customer per day (25). Bottles in their original boxes are exempt.

**Closure override**:
A manual closure entered for emergencies or holidays, with an optional message, start date, and end date, that takes precedence over the normal weekly hours.
_Avoid_: Manual toggle

**Customer**:
A person bringing containers to be redeemed.
_Avoid_: Visitor, client
