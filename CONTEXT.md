# Taunton Redemption Center Website

A one-page public site for a Massachusetts redemption center, telling customers whether the shop is open and what containers and rules apply.

## Language

**Redemption center**:
A shop that takes back empty deposit containers from customers and pays out the deposit, less any processing fee it chooses to deduct.
_Avoid_: Recycling center, bottle shop

**Deposit container**:
A can or bottle that earns the 5¢ Massachusetts deposit: carbonated soft drinks, beer, malt beverages, sparkling water.
_Avoid_: Returnable, recyclable

**Non-deposit container**:
A container that earns no deposit, such as water, juice, iced tea, wine, or liquor bottles. The shop does not accept these.
_Avoid_: Non-returnable

**Daily bottle limit**:
The most loose deposit glass bottles of alcoholic drinks the shop accepts from one customer per day (25). Bottles in their original boxes are exempt.

**Closure**:
A day or range of days the shop will not open, with a reason, entered by hand for emergencies, holidays, or any planned closure. A closure takes precedence over the normal weekly hours. It starts and ends on its listed dates, and a closure with no end date stays in effect until removed.
_Avoid_: Override, manual toggle

**Early close**:
A day the shop opens as usual but closes at a set time, such as a half day. It is entered as a closure with an "open until" time. It can only shorten a day, never extend the normal hours, and in a range of days it applies to every day.

**Upcoming closures notice**:
The strip under the status banner that lists closures that have not ended and start within the next 14 days, each with its day and reason. It still lists a closure on the day it happens, so customers who see it late know why the shop is closed.

**Customer**:
A person bringing containers to be redeemed.
_Avoid_: Visitor, client
